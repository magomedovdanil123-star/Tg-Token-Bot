The historical event-driven 1H discovery uses only closed candles, a 12-bar per-ticker cooldown, conservative same-bar TP/SL handling, and a 0.1% round-trip cost. IMOEX regime is derived causally from the closed 1H index candles. Level research found no eligible bounce setup; the only positive train/test family was a low-sample SHORT after a 0.5% break below a repeatedly tested support, so it remains research-only.

**Why:** Break-support candidates had positive expectancy in both splits, but only 15–17 test trades and no validated bounce edge; this is insufficient evidence for Telegram activation.

**How to apply:** Keep level-break research separate from Smart Money and live Replit. Require a larger out-of-sample sample and stability across periods/tickers before activation. Do not claim the current experimental momentum-volume mode is validated.
