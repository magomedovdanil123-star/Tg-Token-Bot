---
name: Wave signal display
description: Product rule for current Elliott/ABC signal selection in Telegram.
---

The wave section does not require a minimum number of historical analogues before displaying a current structure. It reports how many current signals were found and shows up to five highest-confidence candidates with entry, TP, and SL; historical statistics remain informational.

**Why:** Requiring a small analogue threshold still hid actionable current structures when the latest market shape did not match a positively performing pooled scenario.

**How to apply:** Keep the wave scanner focused on current confirmed Elliott/ABC/Fibonacci structures, deduplicate them by ticker/timeframe/direction/scenario, and cap the Telegram presentation at five.
